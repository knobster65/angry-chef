Our Story

Cooking is
Violence with Purpose

Chef Marco Valtieri learned the knife at his grandmother's side in Bologna, then spent twelve years in Michelin kitchens across Europe. Every scar on his hands tells a story of obsession.

Angry Chef isn't a gimmick — it's philosophy. The fire that drives every dish is real: open flames, screaming pans, and the kind of intensity that only comes when someone truly cares about what lands on your plate.
