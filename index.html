Gameplay

Three Modes. Pure Stress.

Each mode is a different flavor of kitchen panic. Pick one, or play them all — the chefs don't care.

01 🃏

Solitaire

Card-Flipping Stress Test

Strategic card stacking meets screaming chefs. Build towers of culinary cards while angry bosses throw kitchen equipment at you.

02 🀄

Mahjong

Tile Stacking Meltdown

Classic tile-matching with a temper tantrum. Clear the board before the kitchen catches fire — and it always does.

03 💥

Match-2

Kitchen Chaos Mixer

Fast-paced pairing under extreme pressure. Match ingredients, dodge flying pans, survive the rush — it's like Hell's Kitchen on speed.
